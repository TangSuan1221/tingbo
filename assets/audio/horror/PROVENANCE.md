# Horror audio provenance

All files in this folder are licensed under CC0 1.0 and may be used, modified, and redistributed without attribution.

- `lost-in-a-bad-place.ogg` — “Lost in a bad place”, congusbongus, OpenGameArt
  - https://opengameart.org/content/lost-in-a-bad-place-horror-ambience-loop
- `abandoned-passages.ogg` — “Abandoned passages”, congusbongus, OpenGameArt
  - https://opengameart.org/content/abandoned-passages-horror-ambience-loop
- `ambient-horror.ogg` — “Ambient horror”, techiew, OpenGameArt
  - https://opengameart.org/content/ambient-horror
- `abyss-sting.ogg`, `piano-sting.mp3`, and the normalized runtime conversion `piano-sting.ogg` — “String and piano horror stings”, congusbongus, OpenGameArt
  - https://opengameart.org/content/string-and-piano-horror-stings
- `horror-hit-bassy.ogg`, `horror-hit-high.ogg`, and `horror-hit-mid.ogg` — selected and OGG-converted files from “Horror Hit Soundpack 1”, psychhead_, OpenGameArt
  - https://opengameart.org/content/horror-hit-soundpack-1

The local source archive and extraction directory used during selection are temporary working files and are not runtime dependencies.

The `.m4a` files are AAC runtime conversions of the corresponding CC0 sources. The two horror-hit sources and the piano sting were loudness-normalized before conversion so they remain audible over dialogue and ambience.
